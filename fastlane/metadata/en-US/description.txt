HardAlarm is the uncompromising alarm clock built specifically for heavy sleepers, chronic snoozers, and anyone who struggles to break through morning brain fog.

Traditional alarm clocks make it effortless to roll over and tap snooze in a daze. HardAlarm changes the morning equation: the alarm will NOT turn off until you prove you are awake by solving a cognitive puzzle or completing a physical exercise mission.

Choose your challenge, jumpstart your nervous system, and conquer your mornings.

==================================================
WAKE-UP CHALLENGES: PHYSICAL & COGNITIVE
==================================================
When creating an alarm, select exactly one wake-up challenge or let HardAlarm choose a random one day-by-day:

• PUSH-UP COUNTER: Place your phone face up beneath your chest. Real-time proximity sensors detect each repetition. Knock out 10 push-ups to rush oxygen to your brain.
• PHOTO HUNT: Forces you out of bed! Snap a live photo of a morning household object (coffee mug, bathroom sink, shoes, water bottle, laptop, or book) using on-device CoreML object recognition.
• MATH MATCH: Break sleep inertia with dynamic arithmetic equations. Solve missing-number algebra and arithmetic before your ears get a rest.
• MEMORY SEQUENCE: A 4x4 matrix puzzle that displays a glowing color sequence, masks it, and challenges your working memory to identify the target tile.
• MORNING SQUATS: CoreMotion accelerometer sensors detect 10 deep squats, activating your body's largest muscle groups for an instant adrenaline boost.
• VIGOROUS SHAKE: Shake your phone with high kinetic energy until the meter fills.
• STEP WALKOUT: Stand up and walk 20 steps away from your bed to shut off the ringing.
• RANDOM (DAY BY DAY): Automatically rotates your challenge every morning so you never build automated muscle memory.
